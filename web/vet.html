Diagnose my cat

        
A soldering check-up, no firmware needed. I'll connect over USB, look at every pin the way a multimeter would, ask the radio to identify itself, scan the I2C header, then reboot the board and see whether its firmware comes up.

        

          
          
          
          
          
        

        

      

    


    


    

      

      

      

        
        
      

    


    
What the vet can see: rest levels with only the chip's own pull resistors, whether neighbouring module pins are shorted, whether the LoRa radio answers over SPI, I2C pull-ups and devices, the flash and PSRAM fitted, and how the board boots. What it can't: LEDs lighting, microphones, anything that needs firmware timing. Nothing is written to flash.
